Why FairShift exists

Casual work shouldn't mean casual pay

Hospitality runs on casual staff, but most venues still plan by gut feel and WhatsApp. When demand is guessed, both sides lose. The owner is short-staffed on the busy night and overstaffed on the quiet one, and the worker carries the cost: called in late, sent home early, paid days later, tips held until month-end.

FairShift gives the owner a forecast they can act on, and gives the worker terms set before they accept and pay that follows the shift. The AI does the noticing and the checking. People make every decision about work and money.

Scheduling

Called in late, sent home early

Shifts filled by last-minute messages; quiet nights end early with lost hours.

Pay

Paid days or weeks later

Cash in hand or a delayed transfer, with no record of how the amount was worked out.

Tips

Tips held until month-end

Card tips sit with the venue until payroll, even for the lowest-paid staff.

How it works

From tonight's forecast
to money in the wallet

01 · Forecast

The AI sees the busy shift coming

Your sales history, plus weather, holidays and what's happening near each venue. Every heads-up links its source.

02 · Fair offers

You approve, then offers go out

Suggested shifts arrive ready to send. Workers who want more hours are offered first, with rate and deadline on every offer.

03 · Shift worked

Hours confirmed by a person

A manager or the owner confirms the shift. Pay comes from the terms the worker accepted, never from the AI.

04 · Checked

The Pay Agent looks for problems

Overlapping shifts, hours beyond the booking, unusual tips. Anything odd is held for the owner with a plain reason.

05 · Paid

Straight to the PayPal wallet

Shown in rand, paid in US dollars at the locked ECB rate. Marked paid only when PayPal confirms it.

06 · Pathway

Regular hours become visible

When a casual worker passes your hours threshold (for example 20 h a week over 12 weeks), you're alerted to consider a permanent role. FairShift never makes the offer for you.

Fair by design

Five promises that hold on every shift

FairShift isn't payroll, a staffing agency or a job marketplace. It works for venues and the people already on their team, and its job ends when shift earnings and tips reach the worker's PayPal account.

  1. 01

    The AI suggests, people decide

    Every suggestion shows its reason and source. The AI never sets pay, picks a worker or books a shift, and nothing is offered or paid without a person's approval.

  2. 02

    Terms come before the shift

    Rate, offer order and tip rule are shown before anyone accepts, and never change after.

  3. 03

    Pay is never below the floor

    Every offer is checked against the minimum wage for your country and the shift's date.

  4. 04

    Tips belong to the people who earned them

    Allocated under your written venue rule, never reduced to cover costs.

  5. 05

    Workers see only their own numbers

    Their earnings, offers and progress. Nobody else's.
