# FairShift: for judges

- **Live site:** https://getafairshift.com. It runs on the PayPal Sandbox, so no real money moves.
- **Sign-in details** for the live venue, a Sandbox buyer and a Sandbox PayPal app are in the private testing instructions on Devpost. They are deliberately not in this repository.
- **Route 1, run a live venue:** sign in to Jacaranda Table, a fictional five-venue group that keeps trading. See the AI signals and the AI briefing on Plan, approve suggested shifts on Fill shifts, accept an offer as a worker, confirm a finished shift on Pay and watch the Pay Agent pay or hold it, release a held payment with a reason, and check every PayPal and AI call in PayPal & AI records.
- **Route 2, start your own venue:** Start free trial, confirm the email, approve the 30-day trial in PayPal Sandbox, add a venue, add sales history (sample history, or upload [`docs/samples/fairshift-sample-sales.csv`](samples/fairshift-sample-sales.csv)), set pay terms, invite a worker, and connect a PayPal Sandbox app under Business settings.
- **Real:** PayPal Sandbox payouts and subscriptions, OpenAI calls, ECB rates, weather, public-holiday and event-listing feeds. **Sample:** sales history and till tips are generated and labelled synthetic. **Demo:** two labelled DEMO scenarios, a planned water interruption at Rosebank and one worker booked at two venues at once.
- **Evidence** for the submitted build: [`docs/SUBMISSION_EVIDENCE.md`](SUBMISSION_EVIDENCE.md).
